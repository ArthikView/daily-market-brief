DAILY EDITION · FRIDAY · 27 NOVEMBER 2026

Markets retreat as oil and rupee pressure return, while the RBI adjusts liquidity rules

Indian equities snapped a two-session recovery and closed lower as selling returned to IT, auto and financial shares. The rupee weakened to 92.99 per dollar while Brent rose above $96.50. The RBI responded to tighter liquidity conditions by easing the daily minimum CRR maintenance requirement from 99% to 90%, effective 1 December. Global equities, meanwhile, staged a broad rebound as AI-related risk appetite improved.

WHY IT MATTERS · ANALYSIS

The contrast between falling headline indices and positive mid-/small-cap benchmarks suggests the session was not a uniform liquidation across the entire listed market. The weakness in large IT, auto and financial names had greater index impact. The weekly decline also interrupted the recent four-week advance, so the next session's breadth and foreign-flow data matter more than a single headline close.

RBI and domestic macro: CRR flexibility returns

Daily CRR maintenance reduced to 90%

REPORTED FACTS

In its 27 November circular, the RBI reduced the minimum daily maintenance of the cash reserve ratio from 99% of the requirement to 90%, effective from the fortnight beginning 1 December 2026. Read the RBI notification. Reuters also reported that the change follows a shift from surplus to deficit banking-system liquidity.

WHY IT MATTERS · ANALYSIS

This is an operational easing in reserve-management flexibility, not a reduction in the statutory CRR ratio itself and not a policy-rate cut. Banks gain more room to manage reserve balances across the maintenance fortnight. The likely transmission is toward smoother overnight funding conditions, although the effect depends on the distribution of liquidity between banks and the RBI's other operations.

RBI liquidity and government-security operations

Rupee, crude and commodities

REPORTED FACTS

Reuters reported the rupee at ₹92.99 per dollar, down 0.6% on Friday and 0.3% for the week. Brent crude traded above $96.50 and later touched a more-than-two-week high near $98 amid renewed supply-disruption concerns. Sources: Reuters on the rupee and Reuters global markets report.

INDIA TRANSMISSION · ANALYSIS

A weaker rupee and firmer crude can reinforce each other in the import-cost channel: the same dollar oil price translates into a higher rupee bill when the currency depreciates. If sustained, this can affect fuel marketing margins, transport costs, imported-input costs and the current account. The pass-through to retail inflation is neither immediate nor one-for-one; taxes, administered prices, hedging and domestic demand all matter.

Global markets: AI risk appetite rebounds

REPORTED FACTS

Reuters reported Nasdaq futures up 2.1%, S&P 500 futures up 1.5%, European shares up 0.8%, Japan's Nikkei up 2.1% and China's CSI 300 up 4.2%. The US 10-year Treasury yield rose five basis points to 4.90% as equities recovered. The US cash market had been closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving. These are Reuters' Friday snapshots, not final closes. Source: Reuters, 27 November 2026.

WHY IT MATTERS · ANALYSIS

The global rebound shows that risk appetite can recover quickly after technology-led selling, but it does not establish that the valuation debate around AI has ended. For India, global equity flows, the dollar, US yields and oil remain important external channels. A firmer US yield backdrop can compete with emerging-market assets for capital, while a stronger oil price is a separate headwind for India's external balance.

India implications: what to watch next

  • RBI liquidity: watch overnight money-market rates and bank funding conditions as the revised CRR maintenance rule begins on 1 December.
  • Rupee and oil: monitor whether Brent's rise persists and whether currency weakness broadens into imported inflation concerns.
  • Market breadth: check whether large-cap selling spreads to mid- and small-caps, or remains concentrated in a handful of heavyweight sectors.
  • GDP release: MoSPI's advance release calendar schedules the Q2 FY2026-27 quarterly GDP estimates for 30 November 2026. The date is listed in the official 2026-27 release calendar.
  • Next Indian market session: Monday, 30 November 2026, following the weekend. Confirm exchange notices before the session.

Economist's perspective

Friday's market decline and the RBI's CRR adjustment tell two different parts of the same liquidity story. Equity prices reacted to global risk appetite, oil and currency pressure, while the central bank's measure addresses the mechanics of bank reserve management. It should not be read as a change in the policy stance by itself. For the coming week, the most useful signals will be the GDP release, the rupee-oil combination, and whether interbank liquidity conditions stabilise after the new maintenance rule takes effect.
